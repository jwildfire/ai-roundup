Insilico Medicine doses first patient in Phase 3 trial of a generative-AI-designed drug

GENESIS-IPF-3 is described as the world's first Phase 3 trial of a drug both discovered and designed by generative AI (rentosertib, for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis) — a concrete proof point that the AI-drug pipeline has reached late-stage development, not just discovery.

nPhase.ai launches agentic platform for clinical trial data management

The platform claims to map over 90% of raw study data to CDISC standards without human intervention and produce submission-ready output roughly four times faster with 80% fewer resources — a sharp data point for the RBQM/data-quality thread of the talk.

Citeline launches Patient Intelligence for trial feasibility and recruitment planning

Combines patient-level data with Citeline's trial, site, and pipeline intelligence to feed feasibility, site selection, and recruitment workflows — another sign recruitment tooling is consolidating around unified, AI-fed data platforms.

Forbes: agentic clinical AI still lives or dies on data criticality

A sharp, quotable framing for the talk: roughly 80% of the work in building production-grade agentic clinical systems goes into cleaning unstructured trial narratives and standardizing CDISC schemas, not the agent logic itself.

Medidata's NEXT China event signals shift from point AI tools to unified trial platforms

At its Shanghai conference, Medidata argued the era of scattering disconnected AI tools across a study is ending in favor of a single platform running the entire trial — while China simultaneously rewrites its regulatory approval process to fold AI in, a useful macro talking point on where the industry is headed.
